QUARTER-FINAL PREVIEW | 07/11

八強最後兩戰賽前：四強門票與半區壓力

法國與西班牙已先到四強，最後兩張門票留給 England、Norway、Argentina、Switzerland。

這兩場八強戰把淘汰賽最後的變數留在下半區。

01

上半區已定，下半區看風格複製

France and Spain are already through

France beat Morocco 2-0 and Spain beat Belgium 2-1, so one semi-final is already locked. The remaining pressure now sits on the other half of the bracket, where neither tie is about reputation alone. Each match asks whether a team can repeat its best game model under quarter-final stress.

02

England vs Norway：高溫與直線衝擊

Haaland, heat, and midfield control

England arrived after a messy win over Mexico, while Norway came in with Haaland's finishing form and the confidence of eliminating Brazil. Guardian's pre-match coverage framed the tie around England's search for a stable identity and Norway's ability to turn direct attacks into immediate box pressure. In Miami, heat management matters almost as much as shape.

03

Argentina vs Switzerland：先把節奏拉回來

Messi is still there, but game control is the issue

Argentina reached the quarter-finals only after a late comeback against Egypt, which made this tie less about star power and more about control. Switzerland's route suggested a slower, lower-margin match. The key question was whether Argentina could keep the game orderly enough to avoid another chaotic finish.
